Voice range and room noise

About this experiment

Corrected YIN periodicity analysis uses overlapping audio windows, with the bundled CREPE model as a confidence-aware cross-check. Audio processing runs in workers. Uncertain, clipped, and silent frames leave gaps instead of repeating an old pitch. “Pitch evidence” describes periodicity and detector agreement; it is not a probability that a swara is correct.

This is a single-pitch tracker, not a voice separator. A louder drone, another singer, or strong room noise can still mislead it. The bundled CREPE model is the existing compact model. Real-vocal accuracy needs comparison against labelled recordings.
