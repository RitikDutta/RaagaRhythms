WHAT DID YOUR HAND CHANGE?

The sound around
the mic changes.

Your hand can reflect and filter sound near the microphone. That can alter resonances and confuse the pitch tracker into reporting a different harmonic or an out-of-range pitch. That warning is a clue about the sound, not a measurement of distance.

This experiment compares loudness and the balance of low and high frequencies with your two captured samples. Try Hand near the mic to keep your head still and test the exact effect you noticed.

You need to keep humming. A silent person cannot be located this way. Louder humming, a different note, a different room, or another sound can resemble movement; “uncertain” means the samples don’t give a clear answer.
